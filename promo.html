Four steps

Use the guide

  1. Choose federal or state. Senate and House on one side. Governors, and the D.C. mayor, on the other.
  2. Pick a state on the map. Search by name if you don’t want to hunt for it.
  3. Open the district. Every House seat is on the 2026 ballot, including at-large states.
  4. Or type the district. What’s my ballot lists the Senate, House, and governor matchup for that place.

Where the pages come from

Public records, labeled gaps

  • Candidate lists and race notes come from public reporting, including the Wikipedia pages for these elections, as of October 1, 2026.
  • Ratings on the 2026 guide are Cook Political Report labels from that same window.
  • The money bar uses the FEC all-candidates file for federal candidates. State races are not filled from federal reports.
  • Portraits are freely licensed photos from Wikimedia Commons. The photographer and license are printed on the card. Initials appear when no free portrait matched that person and state.
  • A button at the bottom of the 2026 guide loads today’s headlines from NPR, PBS NewsHour, and The New York Times while the app is running.
